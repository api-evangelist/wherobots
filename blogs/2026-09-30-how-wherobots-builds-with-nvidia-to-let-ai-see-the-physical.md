The world and what happens in it is digitized by petabytes of raw and derivative spatial datasets of various data types and scales, and the potential for applying AI to it is immense. But the AI models and agents we use every day need connectivity to tools that turn this data into usable insights and relationships. Wherobots gives AI the ability operate on and understand raw physical-world data, and NVIDIA GPUs are core to it.
